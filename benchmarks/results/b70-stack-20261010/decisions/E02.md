# E02: Graph-active FP16 GDN gated RMSNorm

**Decision: NO_MATERIAL_GAIN — profile gate; not implemented.**

The compiled existing GDN norm is present in captured C1/C4, approximately 0.210/0.247 ms per observed round. Even complete removal is below one percent of the measured 54.3/76.7 ms cycle envelopes. A replacement is not justified by an upstream microbenchmark of a different route.

No new SYCL norm operator or serving benchmark was run. Existing norm-to-Half and gate/HadIn rounding boundaries remain unchanged.

[Reviewed evidence](../P0-route-and-cost-report.md). These decisions retain FP16, MTP3, original M04, 262144 total tokens, 16 admitted sequences, 180W and the qualified scheduler.
